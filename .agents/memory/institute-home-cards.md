---
name: Institute home-card visibility
description: The product rule for showing School and Coaching entry cards on the home experience.
---

Show the School card only when at least one School institute is available, and show the Coaching card only when at least one Coaching institute is available. Keep each category hidden while it has no available institutes.

**Why:** The user requested that empty School or Coaching categories not take up space on the home page.

**How to apply:** When changing these home cards, base visibility on the available institute list for each category, not only on whether the current student is already enrolled.
