---
name: Study Room question timing
description: Product behavior for Study Room timer modes, host selection, and student-controlled Ideal Question Pace & Vibration Alert.
---

1. **Host Setup**:
- In Study Room test creation, the mode is `MIX` by default.
- Mode chooser cards (Per-Question, Total Test Time, Mix) are removed from the host interface.
- Host only selects the Total Exam Duration (minutes: 5m, 10m, 15m, 20m, 30m, 45m, 60m or custom minutes).
- "Ideal Question Pace (Alert)" and "Phone Vibration Alert" are removed from the host setup.

2. **User / Student Control**:
- Ideal Question Pace (Alert) time and Vibration Alert (ON / Mute) are exclusively controlled by each student.
- In the live test view, students have an interactive Pace badge and a direct 1-tap Vibration ON/OFF toggle, plus a settings modal to adjust pace duration (15s, 20s, 30s, 45s, 60s, custom seconds).
- Vibration is ON by default and resets cleanly for every question the student navigates to (via Next, Prev, or Question Grid).
